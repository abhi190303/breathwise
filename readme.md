# 🌿 BreatheWise AI
**Understand Your Air. Protect Your Health.**

Built for **Hack2Skill Challenge 6 – Urban Air Quality Assistant**, BreatheWise AI is an intelligent environmental assistant that translates live air pollution and weather telemetry into personalized health guidance, daily action plans, and AI-powered answers.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **Live Air Quality & Weather Dashboard:** Real-time US AQI, PM2.5, PM10, CO, NO₂, SO₂, O₃, temperature, humidity, and wind speed (defaults to **Bengaluru, India**, with global city search and GPS support).
- **Grok AI Assistant (`grok-4.7`):** Natural-language environmental chatbot powered by xAI's Grok API, backed by an automatic deterministic reasoning engine so the app works even without an API key.
- **Personalized Health Protocols:** Tailored outdoor/indoor activity, exercise intensity, mask, and ventilation advice for 6 user profiles (*General Adult, Child, Senior Citizen, Respiratory Sensitivity, Outdoor Worker, Fitness Enthusiast*).
- **24-Hour & 7-Day Forecasts + Smart Action Plan:** Interactive Chart.js visualizations that automatically detect peak pollution hours and recommend the cleanest Morning, Afternoon, and Evening activity windows.
- **Multi-City Comparison & Smart Alerts:** Side-by-side city air quality comparison, chemical-ratio source insights, and customizable AQI threshold alerts.
